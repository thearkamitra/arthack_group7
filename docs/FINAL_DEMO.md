### Copyable 15-second creative prompt

artwork for Inner Landscape, a participant-led guided reflection experience.
Preserve the reference's soft, grainy, luminous painted texture: dark warm brown
negative space, coral-red flowing ribbons, pale blue and cream spires, suspended
pink and blue ovals, and delicate four-point star shapes. Keep this painterly
material throughout, including close views. No added text or interface.

0–4 seconds: Begin exactly from Picture 1, preserving its composition, aspect
ratio and recognizable forms. Hold a stable horizon and fixed field of view.
Allow only very subtle local shimmering and slow drift. Establish the selected
upper-left pink oval and the nearby pale-blue four-point star without changing
their identity. The visual direction expresses curiosity and room to explore;
do not assign a fixed emotional meaning to any color or shape.

4–11 seconds: Make a smooth, slow forward approach toward that same pink oval.
Bring it gradually toward the center and make it visibly larger over time.
Maintain continuous spatial relationships, gentle parallax, and a stable
horizon. Reveal a few distinct nearby companion forms related to its rounded
silhouette, soft material, pink/blue palette and coral light. Keep these forms
separate and readable as the camera continues inward. Avoid rapid acceleration,
orbiting, camera roll, wide pans, pullback, tunnels, portals and a widening scene.

11–15 seconds: Ease smoothly toward a stable new composition. The approached
oval remains recognizable alongside several related forms, soft blue/cream
structures and warm negative space. End with almost no motion, a sharp legible
image, and enough distinct shapes to invite the next participant choice. If
Picture 2 is supplied, it must be the approved related-form ending and match
exactly at 15 seconds; do not substitute an unrelated ocean or fabric world.
Do not fill the entire ending with featureless macro texture.

Sound, only if the chosen workflow supports it: optional quiet, sparse ambient
tones with ample silence for the spoken guide. No speech, beat, dramatic swell,
heavy underwater rushing or sudden effects. The application controls voice and
pause separately; a generated video does not implement those controls.
