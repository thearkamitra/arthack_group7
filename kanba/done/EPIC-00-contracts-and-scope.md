## 1. Summary of Completed Work

- Established core reflection architecture: 30-second exploration cycle, head-directed attention on curved spatial surface, therapist supervision console, and AI reflection guide.
- Defined strict privacy and clinical safety boundaries:
  - Quest 2 has head-directed attention, NOT eye tracking.
  - Heart rate participates only in pacing/readiness check-ins, NOT emotional or diagnostic inference.
  - Private therapist notes remain strictly out of model context.
  - Synthetic client records for demonstration; no claims of clinical diagnosis or treatment efficacy.
- Defined event models: session states, attention summaries, heart-rate observations, conversation turns, decisions, and media job manifests.
